# Failed Stage 6 Completion Experiments

This archive contains the scripts, logs, and datasets from our attempted "Stage 6" completion-only training phase (Stage 6A, Stage 6B, and Stage 6C). 

These experiments have been abandoned and are removed from the active training pipeline.

## Why was Stage 6 abandoned?
The goal of Stage 6 was to bridge the gap between our "Prompt -> Full-Function" curriculum (Stages 2-5) and the standard "Prompt -> Indented Body Completion" format used by HumanEval. However, completion-only training introduced several severe confounders that corrupted the model's generation quality:

1. **Corrupted Completion Targets**: The Hugging Face dataset extraction often left hanging docstrings, mismatched indentation, and syntactically invalid completions.
2. **Test Boilerplate Contamination**: Because the model was trained on full scripts, completion targets inadvertently included `doctest`, `unittest`, or `if __name__ == "__main__"` blocks, teaching the model to hallucinate test cases instead of returning early.
3. **Eval Bug (`<|im_end|>` ignored)**: The evaluation suite failed to stop generation at the ChatML `<|im_end|>` token, leading to false reports of repetition and syntax collapse.
4. **Raw `exec()` Hang Bug**: Early evaluation scripts used an unsafe `exec()` loop which hung indefinitely on infinite loops generated by the model. (This has been replaced with subprocess isolation with timeout and resource limits. This is not a secure sandbox).
5. **Prompt/Body Recombination Bug**: AST parsers failed when completion bodies were not correctly indented or prepended to their signature prompts before evaluation.
6. **Unstable Mixed Objectives**: Trying to train on a 50/50 mix of full-function natural targets and body-only completions caused sudden format collapse (NaN loss and repetition spikes).

The noisy Hugging Face dataset inspection in Stage 6C was also deemed too messy to proceed.

## Conclusion
The results from these Stage 6 directories **should not be used as architecture evidence**. The SamatNext vs. Transformer baseline comparisons were derived cleanly from Stage 5, where the signal and format were stable. Future work may revisit completion-only training using entirely separate pipelines and strictly decontaminated targets.
